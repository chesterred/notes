Seedance 2.5 review notes: camera control and the 10-second ceiling

2026-11-26 · working notes

Camera prompts are followed more literally than I expected. Asking for a slow orbit produced an actual orbit rather than a vague drift, which is rare. The catch is that complex moves eat the clip budget: a 10-second orbit ends just as it starts to look convincing, so plan for a cut rather than a single continuous reveal.

The Korean-language interface is complete and the docs are clearer than the English translation in places. Pricing tiers are on Seedance 2.5and include a small monthly credit pool, so heavy iteration needs the next tier up.

Character consistency across two shots is still the weak point. The same reference image produced a slightly different jawline in the second generation, enough to be noticeable in a split-screen edit. My workaround is to generate both shots in one pass with a longer prompt and cut the result, which keeps the face stable.

Verdict: best camera control I have used at this price; weakest on cross-shot identity. Good for b-roll, not dialogue scenes.
