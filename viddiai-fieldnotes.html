Viddi AI notes: turning a script into a watchable first cut

2026-11-26 · working notes

Scene splitting is the strongest part. The script was broken into beats that mostly matched how I would cut it, and each beat got a duration proportional to its word count. Voice-over pacing was close to natural, with the usual tell: every sentence lands on the same falling cadence, so a second pass on emphasis is needed.

Export options cover 1080p and vertical formats, and captions are generated automatically with decent accuracy. The free plan adds a small watermark. Plan details live on Viddi AIand the watermark removal is the main reason to upgrade.

Stock matching is hit or miss. Generic lines like "a team discussing a roadmap" pulled usable footage; specific lines about a product feature pulled something unrelated. The fix is to rewrite abstract sentences into concrete visual instructions before generating, which is good script discipline anyway.

Verdict: saves an hour on assembly, not on taste. Best for talking-head explainers and social cutdowns.
