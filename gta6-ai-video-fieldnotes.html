GTA 6 AI video: why most fan trailers look wrong

2026-11-26 · working notes

The first mistake is pacing. GTA trailers breathe: a wide establishing shot, two seconds of stillness, then a cut. AI-generated fan trailers cut every 1.5 seconds because that is what makes individual clips look impressive. String enough of them together and the result reads as a highlight reel, not a trailer.

None of this is a reason not to make them — the format is fun and the tools are good enough to be fun with. It is a reason to spend the time on edit and sound instead of more takes. If you want to see how the current tools compare before starting, GTA 6 AI Video collects examples with the prompts and settings people used.

The second is lighting continuity. Each generated shot is lit for its own drama, so a scene jumps from sunset to overcast to neon within four cuts. Picking one time of day and writing it into every prompt fixes more than any upscale. The third is audio: licensed trailer stings do more for believability than another generation pass.

Verdict: the model is not the bottleneck; pacing, lighting and sound are.
