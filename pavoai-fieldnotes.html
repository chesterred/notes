Pavo AI hands-on: where the free tier stops being enough

2026-11-26 · working notes

The editing workspace is the reason to use it. Trimming, captions and a rough colour pass in one place saved the usual export-import dance, and the caption editor handles line breaks better than most. The generation tools are competent but not class-leading; the value is having them next to the timeline rather than in another tab.

Collaboration is basic — a share link and comments, no version history. For a two-person project that is fine. Plan limits and the export rules are documented on Pavo AI, and the watermark placement is the detail most reviews leave out.

The free tier is a trial, not a workflow: exports are limited and the watermark is placed over the lower third where captions live. That is enough to evaluate the interface and not enough to publish. Rendering is cloud-side, so an unstable connection pauses progress rather than corrupting the file, which is the right failure mode.

Verdict: worth it if you cut short-form video weekly; redundant if you already live in a full NLE.
